# Phone setup

Do steps 1 to 3 at home on Wi-Fi before the trip. After that the app works
fully offline, including audio.

## 1. Install the app

**From the download link** (needs internet once):

1. On the phone, open this link in Chrome:
   `https://github.com/danboz5125/japan-phrasebook/releases/latest/download/JapanPhrasebook.apk`
2. Chrome may warn that the file can be harmful. Tap **Download anyway**.
3. Open the downloaded file. Android asks to allow installs from Chrome:
   tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, go back, tap **Install**.
4. Google Play Protect may say it doesn't recognize the developer. Tap
   **More details → Install anyway**. It's a personal app, not from the Play Store.

**From a friend's phone** (no internet needed):

1. On the phone that has the app: **Home → Share app with a friend**
   (or Settings → Share this app), then pick **Quick Share** and the other phone.
2. On the receiving phone, accept the file, open it, and follow steps 3 and 4 above
   (the "allow installs" prompt will name the Files app instead of Chrome).

## 2. Install the Japanese voice (for audio offline)

The app speaks through the phone's own Google text-to-speech. The Japanese
voice must be downloaded once:

1. In the app: **Settings → Voice settings** (or the red banner on Home).
2. Or in Android Settings: **System → Languages → Text-to-speech output →
   Speech Services by Google (gear icon) → Install voice data → Japanese**.
3. Download it, then come back to the app. Settings should say
   **Japanese voice ready**. Tap **Test: Konnichiwa** to hear it.
4. Turn on airplane mode and tap a phrase to confirm it still speaks.

## 3. Add your hotels

**Mine → Where I'm staying**: paste each hotel's name and Japanese address
(Google Maps or the booking email has it). The card shows the address with
"please take me to this address", ready for a taxi driver.

## Using it

- **Tap any phrase** to hear it. **Tap again** within a few seconds to hear it slowly.
- **★** saves a phrase to Favorites. **Practice** drills your favorites or any situation.
- Blue **They say** phrases are what staff will say to you, with how to answer.
- Search understands English, romaji with or without long-vowel marks
  (`arigato`, `arigatou`, `arigatō`), and Japanese.
- **Mine → Backup → Copy backup** copies your favorites, phrases and hotels as
  text, if you ever want to move them to another phone.

## Updating

Open the same download link again and install over the top. Favorites, your
phrases and hotels are kept. Each phone keeps its own favorites and phrases.
